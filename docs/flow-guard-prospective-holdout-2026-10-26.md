## Frozen experiment

Six synthetic domain labels, four variants each; six records per snapshot and four shuffled constraint types (numeric maximum/minimum/equality and strict boolean equality), plus numeric min/max ranking. Two records are eligible, four attractive traps each violate a different rule; six fixtures have an optimal tie. All traps remained in every ordinary catalog (144/144). Units are already normalized; these are new combinations within the supported grammar, not new NLP/unit support or a real-world corpus.

The actual frozen local WARDEN 0.22.0 SDK buffered final output. Deepseek-flash, temperature .3, thinking disabled, JSON-object mode, maximum 700 tokens; no silent candidate edits. Natural populations have fresh first proposals and at most two repairs. Controlled incorrect initial seeds consume the initial proposal and leave two live repairs. Source-instruction notices use the known injected-arm label, not independent detector evidence.

All predictions met: natural first >=84/96 (89), clean final >=46/48 (48), controlled final >=40/48 (45). These observed transitions have no no-review control for a net causal guard-effect estimate.

## What the failures changed locally

All five unresolved live cases ended in `not-optimal`. For example, the chosen eligible record had value 87 while another eligible record had 84 under `min`. Feedback named the error code but did not give this comparison. After freezing/reporting these outcomes, a separate local change adds selected-vs-best numeric facts, a safe attaining reference, and bounded missing-eligible-reference evidence (counts plus at most eight references). No raw source names, IDs, labels or quotes are echoed. Validation rules, schema, bounds, decisions and final output remain unchanged.

The post-hoc change passed Linux build/typecheck, **798 tests in 24 suites**, including 50 gate/stdio tests, and the 28-scenario red-team gate. Offline replay of **210 proposals / 156 trajectories** preserves every decision and final frame, adding 31 ranking witnesses and 12 missing-set witnesses. No fresh model call tested these new messages: **improved model repair efficacy is unmeasured**. Earlier frozen outcomes are not replaced by replay. Local 0.22.0 remains unpublished.
